This file contains the pattern for updating sections to alternate left/right:

Painter (LEFT): Title/Description on left, Content on right
Animator (RIGHT): Content on left, Title/Description on right
AI Engineer (LEFT): Title/Description on left, Content on right
Developer (RIGHT): Content on left, Title/Description on right
Game Designer (LEFT): Title/Description on left, Content on right

Each uses flex-row or flex-row-reverse to switch sides.
